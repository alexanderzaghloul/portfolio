01 / THESIS

Financial products give people holdings, counterparties, statements, risk metrics, and controls.

Personal data is economically important and widely distributed—yet people rarely receive an equivalent system for understanding the whole relationship.

THE CORE QUESTION

What if you managed your personal data like an investment portfolio?

02 / PRODUCT

A FINANCE-NATIVE INTERACTION MODEL

One interface for the entire relationship.

01

Portfolio

Counterparties, categories, modeled exposure, concentration, and risk in one coherent view.

02

Controls

Turn deletion, export, opt-out, and permission changes into trackable actions—not scattered settings.

03

Statement

04

Consent Ledger

Permission as a transaction: information, purpose, duration, state, and history.
