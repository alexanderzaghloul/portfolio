A LITTLE CONTEXT

Letters become numbers.

Gematria is a Jewish interpretive tradition that explores the numerical values of Hebrew letters and words. This explorer makes the arithmetic visible.

Hebrew · Standard value

א–ט are 1–9, י–צ are 10–90 in tens, and ק–ת are 100–400 in hundreds. Final forms ך, ם, ן, ף, ץ keep the values of כ, מ, נ, פ, צ. Vowel marks, punctuation, spaces, and digits do not contribute.

English · A1–Z26

A = 1, B = 2, through Z = 26. This is a separate English letter-number exercise, not traditional Hebrew gematria. For example, EXCEL adds up to 49.

What does a connection mean?

The examples come from a small, curated word list, not a full Hebrew dictionary. Equal totals are invitations to explore; they do not prove that two words have the same meaning. Hebrew spellings and transliterations can change a result.
