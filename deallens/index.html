05

Investment questions

Methodology & limitations

Equity purchase price = target shares × unaffected price × (1 + premium). Enterprise value = equity purchase price + target debt − target cash. New shares = stock consideration ÷ buyer share price.

Recurring net income = buyer NI + target NI + (realized synergies − new debt interest − foregone cash interest − incremental amortization) × (1 − tax rate). EPS = net income ÷ combined shares. Year-one EPS deducts one-time fees without a tax benefit.

Break-even reports the nonnegative realized pre-tax synergy amount required for EPS neutrality, before one-time fees. It is unavailable at a 100% tax rate.

Illustrative educational model, not a real transaction or investment recommendation. Excludes purchase accounting beyond the entered amortization, debt refinancing, fees financing costs, regulatory constraints, capacity checks, integration timing, tax complexity, and a full balance-sheet/cash-flow model. A 25-case sensitivity is not a forecast. No live market data, employer information, external requests or telemetry.
