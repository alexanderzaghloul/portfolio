Source & reconciliation

Measurement contract

MeasureDefinition and denominator
Paid turnaroundPaymentDate − AcctgDate in calendar days. AcctgDate is the system-entry proxy used by this demo; confirm the correct source field before adapting it. Mean, median and P90 use valid paid rows.
Within thresholdValid elapsed time ≤ selected threshold, divided by all valid assessed rows. Unpaid rows use age at the selected as-of date. Invalid timing is excluded.
Timing exceptionsPaid outside threshold are historical exceptions. Unpaid overdue is current aging within the loaded snapshot. Date/status review requires source validation.
Record grainSource voucher/payment rows, not unique claims. Repeated identical active rows are reported, not silently removed. Source record numbers count parsed CSV records including the header.
Amounts and datesSigned BaseGrossAmount assumed CAD for the loaded Canada export. Calendar dates use UTC arithmetic. P90 uses the nearest-rank definition. Entry cohorts reflect extract status, not historical month-end snapshots.
Scope awaiting confirmationClient invoice and cash collection KPIs are excluded. Their matching rules and source dates need confirmation. No approved success-rate target is supplied.

What this prototype already does

  • Parses new Payment CSVs and validates required fields before loading.
  • Calculates paid timing, distribution statistics and threshold assessments consistently.
  • Separates actionable unpaid aging from historical payment delays.
  • Records and exports exception owners, causes, next steps and review dates.
  • Produces filtered KPI exports and a source-linked audit summary.

How to prove the efficiency benefit

During a pilot, record the time needed to prepare the report and triage exceptions using the current method, then repeat with this tool.

Proposed pilot measures
Preparation minutes per cycle · exceptions assigned an owner · follow-ups completed by review date · recurring exception causes.

No time saving, cost reduction or payment improvement has been measured yet. This is a proposal for validation, not a promised outcome.

Boundaries for a team rollout

This version runs locally, with browser-local follow-up notes. It has no shared multi-user database, PeopleSoft connection or enterprise access controls. A production rollout would need an agreed owner and the organization’s implementation process. Invoice and collection KPIs require their own confirmed source dates and definitions.
