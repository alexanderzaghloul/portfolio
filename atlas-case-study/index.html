01 / THE BUILD STORY

From reporting artifact
to operating system.

01

Find the friction

KPI views and financial follow-up lived across separate files, systems, dates, owners, and definitions. A number could be correct and still fail to create action.

02

Model the workflow

I mapped the path from source record to validation, exception, owner, resolution, and export—then designed each view around the next decision.

03

Build the prototype

I independently developed an interactive prototype with JavaScript, Python, HTML, and CSS, iterating from stakeholder feedback and real operating questions.

04

Test the idea

The work was presented internally and evaluated for continued development. The strongest signal was not a cosmetic dashboard; it was clearer ownership and traceability.

03 / WHAT THE PROJECT TAUGHT ME

Good finance software does not replace judgment.

It gives judgment a reliable place to begin. The most valuable design decisions were the ones that made definitions explicit, preserved the supporting record, distinguished status from outcome, and made ownership visible.

  • Traceability over decorationA metric needs a route back to the record.
  • Exceptions over averagesOperational value often lives in the tail.
  • Ownership over alertsA flag without a next action is only noise.
